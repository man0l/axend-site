A round face has width and length that are close to equal, with soft cheeks and a rounded jaw. The goal of a haircut is not to hide this, since there is nothing to hide, but to add vertical length and a bit of structure so the proportions look more balanced. Here is how.

## Check that your face is actually round

Pull your hair back and photograph yourself straight on. If the width at the cheekbones is close to the length from hairline to chin, and your jaw curves rather than angles, your face is round. If you have a defined jaw, you may be square, and a broader overview is in [face shape and hairstyles for men](/guides/face-shape-hairstyles-men).

## The three principles

1. **Add height on top.** Lifted hair makes the face look longer.
2. **Keep the sides tight.** Volume at the sides adds width, which is what you are trying to avoid.
3. **Use texture and angles.** Sharp parts, defined lines and a textured finish counter softness.

## Haircuts that work

### Textured quiff

Short sides with two to three inches of textured length on top, pushed up and slightly back. It adds height without looking stiff.

### Pompadour or modern pomp

A more dramatic lift. Works well with a fade on the sides. Needs more styling time and product, so be honest about whether you will do it daily.

### Side part with a high or mid fade

The part adds an asymmetrical line, which breaks up the roundness. A fade keeps the sides narrow.

### Faux hawk or textured spikes

Height down the centre and short sides give a strong vertical line. Keep it understated, not extreme.

### Longer top, swept back

If your hair is thick enough, brushing it up and back creates length. Use a light-hold product so it does not look plastered.

## Fades and sides

A mid or high fade with a clean taper keeps the width down. Low fades can work too if you keep enough height on top. Avoid blunt cuts that leave bulk above the ears.

## What to avoid

- **A flat, even buzz cut:** Shows the whole round shape with no vertical element. Fine if you like it, but it does not add structure.
- **A heavy, straight fringe:** Cuts the face horizontally and shortens it.
- **Big side volume:** Adds width.
- **Long hair that falls to the cheeks:** Frames the roundest part of the face.

## Facial hair and round faces

A short beard fuller at the chin and trimmed shorter on the cheeks creates the impression of a longer, more defined lower face. Keep the cheek line neat, and avoid beards that widen the face at the sides. If you cannot grow a full beard, clean stubble or a clean shave is fine, so just keep it tidy.

## Other factors

Face shape is only a starting point. Your hair texture and density matter as much. Curly hair naturally adds width, so a tighter cut on the sides with curls kept on top works. Thinning hair usually looks better shorter. If hair loss concerns you, a doctor or dermatologist can advise.

Body-fat level also changes how round a face looks, though bone structure sets the limits. You do not need to lose weight for a haircut to work, and any weight change should be healthy and gradual.

## Talking to your barber

- Bring photos of men with similar hair type.
- Ask for "short sides, length and texture on top."
- Ask how to style it and what product to use.
- Book regular trims every three to four weeks, since fades grow out quickly.
