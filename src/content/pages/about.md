Axend is a grooming and self-care app for iOS, and this site is its free front door: private face scans and practical guides.

## What we believe

- **Honest numbers.** We show what a scan can measure and what it cannot. We do not flatter and we do not shame.
- **Private by default.** The web scan runs on your device. Your photo is never uploaded.
- **Self-care, not surgery.** Our guides cover grooming, hair, skin routines, posture and presentation. We do not give medical advice and we do not cover surgery.
- **For adults.** The scan and the app are for people 18 and over.

## Editorial standards

Guides are written to be specific and realistic, with no invented statistics or results. When something is a judgment call, we say so. When a topic needs a professional, such as persistent skin problems, we say that too. Posts show their last updated date, and we update them when something changes.
