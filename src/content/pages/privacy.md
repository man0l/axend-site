This page covers the axendai.com website. The Axend iOS app has its own privacy policy, linked from its App Store listing.

## Your photo

When you use a face scan on this site, the photo is processed **entirely in your browser**. It is not uploaded, transmitted, stored or shared, and we cannot see it. It exists in your device's memory while the page is open and is gone when you close or leave the page. The face-landmark model and its runtime are served from this site, and the site's content security policy blocks the scan page from sending data to other origins.

## Age

The scan is for adults 18 and over. You must confirm this before choosing a photo. We do not collect your age or any identity details.

## What we collect

- **No accounts and no sign-up.** We do not ask for your name or email to use the scan.
- **Basic, cookie-less analytics.** We plan to use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies or cross-site tracking and does not need a consent banner.
- **Server logs.** Our host, Cloudflare, processes standard request data (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver and secure the site.

## Links to the App Store

Buttons that open the App Store include a campaign token that identifies which page the click came from. It does not identify you. What happens after that is governed by Apple and the Axend app's own policy.
